Last Student Login Know More Student Apply for Education Loan The applicant must register and login to PM-Vidyalaxmi portal and then fill-up the Common Education Loan Application Form by providing all the necessary details. After filling the form, the applicant can search for Educational Loan and APPLY as per his/her needs, eligibility and convenience. Once student completes the process, application is routed to the selected banks for processing. After sanctioning and Disbursement of the Education Loan Bank shall update the status of loan on VLP as Sanctioned/Disbursed. Student shall receive SMS on status of application. Student may check the status of their application on Dash Board. Student who shall get interest subvention benefit based on eligibility criteria, interest subvention will go to Digital Rupee Wallet and student has to redeem the wallet to his/her loan account. Bank Login Know More Bank Disbursement Process The Bank will update the status of the application on PM-Vidyalaxmi portal. Students can view the status of the application on the applicant's dashboard on the portal. Loan amount will be directly remitted to the account of Educational Institution. The disbursement done by the member bank is outside the purview of the PM-Vidyalaxmi Portal. Status of disbursement done by the bank can be viewed on Dash board by the Students. Ministry Login Know More Ministry of Education Interest Subvention Depending on the details entered, the student will be notified about the eligibility to the interest subvention. On Sanction of the loan and Disbursement, bank will notify the student to claim the interest subvention through SMS/Email/WhatsApp channels. The student has to login to the portal and claim the interest subvention. Ministry of Education will shortlist student who shall get interest subvention benefit based on eligibility criteria. Interest subvention will go to Digital Rupee Wallet and student has to redeem the wallet to his/her loan account. 3 simple steps to apply for an educational loan PM-Vidyalaxmi is a Unified Portal for students who want to apply for Education Loan & Interest Subvention. Registration Students want to apply for education loan on PM-Vidyalaxmi portal, you must register on portal. Please provide essential details as mentioned in registration form. Fill Up Single Form Students have to fill Common Education Loan Application Form by providing all the necessary details. After filling the form, the applicant can search for Educational Loan and APPLY as per his/her needs, eligibility and convenience. Submit Application to Bank Students can submit application to bank for Educational Loan as per his/her needs, ease and convenience. General Details PM-Vidyalaxmi is an initiative that stems out of the National Education Policy (NEP 2020). The policy recommended that financial assistance should be made available to meritorious students through various measures in both public and private HEIs. See Details Credit Guarantee Under this scheme, students can avail loans of up to Rs 7.5 lakh with a 75% credit guarantee, making it easier for students to get loans without collateral or guarantors. See Details Interest Subvention For students with up to Rs. 8 lakhs annual family income, the scheme will also provide for 3% interest subvention on loans up to Rs 10 lakh. See Details Full Interest Subvention Students from families earning up to Rs 4.5 lakh annually will receive full interest subvention. See Details Eligible Institute/College The scheme will be available to students enroling in the Eligible Quality Higher Education Institutions (QHEIs) in India, as per the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF). See Details Simplified Digital Process Applications for loans and interest subventions are processed through a unified, student-friendly PM-Vidyalaxmi portal. The system is fully digital, ensuring transparency and ease of use. Disbursements are made via Digital Rupee wallet. See Details Education loan schemes with Leading Banks in India Leading Banks in India (Scheduled Banks/Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)/Cooperative Banks) are instrumental in helping students achieve their academic goals by providing extensive education loans. These loans cater to both domestic and international studies.
